The project stopped building separate Fedora CoreOS images and included ZFS in all uCore images, with and without NVIDIA drivers.

The existing ZFS-specific tags continued at the time. A later December 2025 announcement retired those redundant tags.

The maintainers reaffirmed their commitment in [“uCore: Streamlining (not retiring)”](https://universal-blue.discourse.group/t/ucore-lets-streamline-not-retiring/9098).
