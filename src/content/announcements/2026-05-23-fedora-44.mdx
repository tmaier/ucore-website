uCore builds moved to Fedora 44 after Fedora CoreOS stable made the same transition.

The `lts` stream moved from longterm kernel 6.12 to longterm 6.18 because the upstream kernel provider did not build 6.12 for Fedora 44. Stable and testing continue to follow their respective stock Fedora CoreOS kernels.
