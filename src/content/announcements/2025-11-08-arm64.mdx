Starting with the November 8, 2025 builds, published manifests selected the appropriate image for x86_64 or aarch64.

At launch the project noted limited direct testing, differences in firmware and architecture-specific packages, and the temporary lack of `mergerfs` on aarch64. The November 20 announcement subsequently reported `mergerfs` availability for both architectures.
